## Campaign and progression

12 levels in four regions, 3 crossings per level. Level-complete screen's primary action is Next Level; replay is secondary. Save unlocks, current level, earned stars and bests locally. No automatic return to level one. Completed levels can be replayed from the journey map. Broken/tampered storage cannot unlock arbitrary indexes or break loading.

Each decision presents three neutral hooks, with balanced correct positions and varied planar/spatial measurements. First level teaches inspect/commit and rope reach; later regions introduce height, lateral displacement, larger rope equipment and mixed triangles. Exactly authored world units remain authoritative.

The flight helper remains explicitly assisted: solved initial impulse, hook-directed pull, automatic continuous-velocity release, gravity, actual floor contact. No typed-answer quiz, arbitrary lives, manual aiming or release precision gate.
